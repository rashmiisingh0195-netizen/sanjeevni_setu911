From symptom to solution, in three steps

  1. 1

    Describe what you feel

    By app, or by sending an SMS if there's no smartphone or internet nearby. Available in Hindi and regional languages.

  2. 2

    Get an honest risk level

    Low, medium, or high — with plain next steps. No jargon, no vague "consult a doctor" with nothing else.

  3. 3

    Get connected to real care

    Nearest open clinic, medicine stock, or a teleconsultation booking — whichever the moment calls for.

Built for the gaps other apps leave open

Works without a smartphone

An SMS/IVR fallback means the platform reaches people on basic feature phones — not just those with data plans, which excludes a huge share of rural India from most health apps today.

Speaks the patient's language

Symptom input and results are available in regional languages, removing the English-literacy barrier that quietly locks most people out of digital health tools.

Digital health record that travels

A patient's history follows them — so a doctor in a different town isn't starting from zero, and repeat tests aren't wasted money.

Early-warning map for health authorities

Anonymised, aggregated symptom clusters are plotted geographically, so local health officers can spot a possible outbreak days before it would otherwise surface.
